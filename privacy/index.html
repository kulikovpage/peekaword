Privacy Policy

Peekaword: Vocabulary Widget · Updated October 3, 2026

In short

Peekaword collects no personal data. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Data on your device

Settings, learning progress, daily goal and streak are stored only on your iPhone and shared between the app and its widgets. Deleting the app deletes them. We have no access to them.

Pronunciation

Words are spoken by the voices built into iOS, on your device.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications created on your iPhone. They are optional and can be turned off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Purchases

The optional subscription is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email or payment details: the app only checks on your device whether the subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash and performance reports. They contain no personal information.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
